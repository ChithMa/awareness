Warning signs of a phishing message

Pay attention to what makes a message suspicious before you react.

📩

Unexpected alerts

Be skeptical of sudden security warnings or login prompts that arrive without notice.

⚠️

Scary language

Attackers often use fear and urgency to push you into acting without thinking.

🌍

Suspicious links

Hover over URLs and look for mismatched domains, strange spellings, or hidden redirects.

🔑

Requests for sensitive data

Legitimate organizations rarely ask for passwords or personal details through email or pop-ups.

How to respond safely

Use these steps to avoid falling victim to phishing attempts.

  1. Stop and review. Don't let urgency override common sense.
  2. Verify the sender. Check the email address or message source carefully.
  3. Hover before clicking. Confirm the real destination of links before you follow them.
  4. Report suspicious content. Let your security team know if something looks off.
